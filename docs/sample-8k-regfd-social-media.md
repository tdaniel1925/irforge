## Item 7.01 — Regulation FD Disclosure

**Designation of Social Media Channel for Disclosure of Material Information**

[REGISTRANT NAME] (the "Company") is filing this Current Report on Form 8-K to notify investors, the market, and the media that the Company intends to use, and may use, the following channel as a means of disclosing information about the Company, its business, and its financial condition, which may be deemed material:

- The Company's official X (formerly Twitter) account, **[@HANDLE]**, located at **https://x.com/[HANDLE]**.

The posts to this X account are, and will be, created and published through the Company's investor-relations platform provided by **PubcoZone (pubcozone.com)**. Posts published through PubcoZone are drafted from the Company's public disclosures and filings, are reviewed and approved by an authorized officer of the Company before publication, and carry the Company's standard disclosures.

The Company also intends to continue to use its website, **[https://www.companywebsite.com]**, and its public company page on PubcoZone at **[https://pubcozone.com/t/[TICKER]]**, as channels for the disclosure of such information.

Investors, analysts, members of the media, and other interested parties are encouraged to review the information the Company posts through these channels, in addition to the Company's filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC"), press releases, and public conference calls and webcasts. The Company will continue to comply with its disclosure obligations under Regulation FD and will continue to use SEC filings, press releases, and public conference calls and webcasts to disclose material, nonpublic information in compliance with Regulation FD.

The information the Company posts through the channels identified above may be deemed material. Accordingly, investors should monitor these channels in addition to the Company's SEC filings, press releases, and public conference calls and webcasts. This announcement is made in reliance on, and in a manner consistent with, the SEC's guidance in its Report of Investigation issued under Section 21(a) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (Release No. 69279, April 2, 2013) regarding the use of company social media channels to disseminate material, nonpublic information (Reg FD), which permits companies to use social media channels to distribute such information provided that investors have been alerted about which channels will be used.

The channels the Company uses may change from time to time, and the Company may add, discontinue, or change the channels it uses. Information contained on, or that can be accessed through, the Company's website, PubcoZone page, or X account is not incorporated by reference into, and is not a part of, this report or any other filing the Company makes with the SEC.

The information in this Item 7.01 is being **furnished** and shall not be deemed "filed" for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the "Exchange Act"), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing.
