# Welcome to PubcoZone — Company Setup Guide

This guide gets your company live in about 10 minutes. PubcoZone is your investor-
relations program in one place: turn your SEC filings into compliant posts you
approve, answer investors on the record, run an IR CRM, and defend your name —
all with your team.

**The golden rule:** nothing ever posts without a named human's approval, and your
disclosures attach to every post automatically. You and your counsel decide what's
safe; we publish what you approve.

---

## 1. Get into your account

There are two ways you arrive:

- **You signed up** at pubcozone.com as a *company* account → your company is
  created and you're its first **admin**.
- **You were given free access** → click the link in your invitation email, sign up
  with that same email, and you're connected to your account as **admin**.

> An **admin** sets up the company and manages the team. A **member** uses the shared
> dashboard plus their own private workspace. You can add either later.

---

## 2. Run the setup wizard (5 steps)

The first time you sign in you'll go through a short wizard:

1. **Ticker** — Type your stock symbol. We pull your company profile from SEC EDGAR
   automatically. (No EDGAR record? OTC and non-US are fine — just type your details.)
2. **Confirm** — Review the auto-filled name, sector, description, and peer tickers.
   Fix anything that's off.
3. **Approver** — Name the person who signs off on posts (CFO, IR lead). Nothing
   publishes without their approval.
4. **Compliance** — Your disclosure and forward-looking-statements language. It's
   pre-filled and attached to every post automatically; edit it if your counsel
   prefers different wording.
5. **Plan** — Pick a plan. No credit card is needed to get started.

When you finish, PubcoZone pulls your real filings and **drafts your first posts**
for you, so your **Home** inbox isn't empty. Review and approve them whenever you're
ready — they only go out when you tap approve.

---

## 3. Finish company setup (admins)

Open **Get started** (top of the left menu) — it's a live checklist that shows what's
done and what's left, and it checks items off automatically as you complete them.

Most company-wide setup is in **Settings** (top-right profile menu → ⚙ Settings):

- **Connect your social accounts** — Settings → "Your social accounts" → **Connect**.
  This links *your* X / LinkedIn / Facebook / etc. Approved posts publish to these.
- **Add peer tickers** — powers Fund Finder (which investors hold companies like
  yours) and peer comparisons.
- **Disclosures & Quiet Mode** — adjust your compliance language; turn on Quiet Mode
  around earnings or financings to pause all publishing in one tap.
- **Billing** — manage your plan anytime.

---

## 4. Invite your team

Go to **Team** (top-right profile menu → Team) and invite people by email:

- **Admin** — can configure the company and manage users.
- **Member** — uses the shared company dashboard, plus their own CRM ownership and a
  private workspace only they can see.

Everyone shares the same company data; each person also gets a private scratchpad.

---

## 5. Everyone's quick personal setup

Each person (admins and members) finishes a short personal checklist on **Get
started**: approve a first post, set up their workspace, and skim the 2-minute IR
primer in **Public Company 101**.

---

## What you can do once you're set up

- **Home** — your approval inbox; approve / edit / skip drafted posts.
- **Content Pipeline & Writing Studio** — turn any topic or filing into a post in
  your executives' voice, screened by the Reg FD check.
- **Counsel Console** — route anything flagged as potentially material to counsel
  for a signed, on-the-record sign-off.
- **CRM & Investor Inbox** — track every contact and inbound message; AI drafts
  replies grounded in your public record.
- **Find Investors** — real funds (from SEC 13F data) that hold your peers, with
  contact paths and a ready intro note.
- **Defend Your Name** — flagged threats/FUD with a calm, filing-cited rebuttal
  ready to approve.
- **Your Public Page** — a verified, trustworthy investor page where you answer
  questions on the record.

Questions? Email support, or open **Learn → How setup works** inside the app.
