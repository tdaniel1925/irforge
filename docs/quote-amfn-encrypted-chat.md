## What's included

**Encryption & security**
- **AES-256 encryption at rest** — message contents are encrypted in the database;
  stored data is unreadable without the company key.
- **Encrypted in transit** — all traffic over TLS (HTTPS), end to end between
  browser and server.
- **Per-company encryption key** — your messages are encrypted with a key unique
  to American Fusion; no other company can read them.

**Access control**
- **IP-address allowlisting** — members can only enter the encrypted chat from
  approved IP addresses (e.g. office + each member's home/phone), so access is
  locked to known locations.
- **User-gated rooms** — admins choose exactly who can join each encrypted room.
- **Separate secure rooms** — e.g. one for Investor Relations, one for Tech &
  Development, isolated from each other.

**Audit & oversight**
- **Append-only audit log** — every join, message, and access attempt is logged
  with user, timestamp, and IP for a defensible record.
- **Admin controls** — add/remove members and manage room access from inside the
  app.

---

## What this is (and isn't)

- This is **server-side AES-256 encryption with IP-gated, audited, user-controlled
  access** — the practical, robust standard for secure internal team chat (the same
  AES-256 standard used by banks and HTTPS).
- It is **not** device-only end-to-end encryption (the Signal model, where even the
  server can't read messages). That's a larger, separate engagement; if you need
  device-level E2EE specifically, we can quote it separately.
